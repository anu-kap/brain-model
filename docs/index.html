What we do

Attention is measurable, not mysterious.

Every second of a video produces a response in the viewer's brain. We simulate that response for your footage and read out what matters: whether people stop, whether they stay, and when they leave.

The result is a score and a second-by-second trace for each variant, delivered in minutes. No panels, no surveys, no waiting for a campaign to tell you what you could have known on day one.

How it works

Three steps from upload to decision.

  1. Upload your variants

    Drop in cuts, hooks, thumbnails or full edits. Anything from five seconds to a minute, in any format your editor exports.

  2. We simulate the viewer

    A brain model trained on thousands of hours of recorded neural activity predicts how visual, auditory and language cortex respond to each frame.

  3. You ship the winner

    Variants are ranked by predicted engagement, with a trace that shows exactly where attention peaks and where it falls away.

Creative testing has always meant guessing, then paying to find out. We want it to mean knowing.
