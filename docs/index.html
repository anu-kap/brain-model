What we do

Attention is measurable, not mysterious.

Every second of a video produces a response in the viewer's brain. We simulate that response for your footage and read out what matters: whether people stop, whether they stay, and when they leave.

The result is a score and a second-by-second trace for each variant, delivered in minutes. No panels, no surveys, no waiting for a campaign to tell you what you could have known on day one.

How it works

From ad variants to viewer retention.

Scroll sideways to follow the pipeline →

Each ad is broken down into what viewers see, hear and are told. We estimate how the brain is likely to respond to it, then use an engagement model trained on real viewing data to predict retention. Once ads go live, their results feed back in, so predictions keep improving.

  1. Upload your variants

    Drop in cuts, hooks or full edits, anything from five seconds to a minute, before a dollar goes to distribution.

  2. We model the viewer

    Each ad is broken into visuals, sound and speech. A brain response model estimates how a viewer's brain reacts to each second, and an engagement model trained on real viewing data turns that into retention.

  3. You ship the winner

    Variants are ranked by predicted retention, with a drop-off curve and plain-language reasons for where attention holds and where it falls away.

What you get

Three answers, before launch.

01  RETENTION SCORE

Pick the winner before you spend

Compare every variant side by side and put your budget behind the one most likely to hold attention.

02  DROP-OFF CURVE

See where viewers drop off

A second-by-second curve, with extra detail on the first few seconds, where most short-form ads lose people.

03  WHY

Know what to change

Clear reasons tied to moments in the ad, such as a hook that lands too late or speech that starts after viewers have moved on.
